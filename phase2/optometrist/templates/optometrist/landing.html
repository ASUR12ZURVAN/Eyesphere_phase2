Helpful information

Support & FAQs

Quick answers to help you get started with NetraScreen.

Which portal should I use?

Doctors use the Doctor Portal, eye-care professionals use the Optometrist Portal, and individuals use the Patient Portal.

How do we arrange a corporate eye camp?

Send an enquiry using the corporate form above. Our team can discuss screening options for your organization.

How do patients register at a camp?

Scan the camp registration QR code to open the screening registration form on a phone.

Need help accessing your account?

Use the portal that matches your role. For account support, contact your organization's NetraScreen administrator.

We're here to help

For corporate eye camps, send us an enquiry above. For portal access, please contact your organization's administrator.
